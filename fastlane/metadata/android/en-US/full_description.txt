Essential Moments is a private, completely offline AI-powered gallery search application for Android.

Search your personal photo collection using natural language descriptions like "cat sitting in a box", "sunset on the beach", or "documents on a desk".

<b>Key Features:</b>
• 100% On-Device AI: Runs quantized SigLIP 2 vision-language models locally using ONNX Runtime.
• Absolute Privacy: Your photos, metadata, and embeddings never leave your device. Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero ads.
• Natural Language Photo Search: Find photos by visual concepts without relying on tags, file names, or manual folders.
• Background Indexing: Efficiently indexes photos in the background using Android WorkManager and AndroidX Room.

<b>Requirements:</b>
• Android 11 (API level 30) or higher.
• Initial model download requires approximately 380 MB of storage.

<b>Permissions:</b>
• Photos / Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to index and display photos from your device gallery. On supported Android versions, you can choose full gallery access or select specific photos.
• Internet Access (INTERNET): Used exclusively to download the quantized AI model weights and verify the manifest on initial setup. No data or photos are ever transmitted externally.
